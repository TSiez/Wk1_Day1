How it works

From folder to findings, in three moves.

Tester.io plugs into the documents you already have. You set the standard. We do the reading.

Connect

02

Review

Run a review against your policy library, a regulatory framework, or a previous deal as the standard. Tester.io reads in seconds, not weeks.
